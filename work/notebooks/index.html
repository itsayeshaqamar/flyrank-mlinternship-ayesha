07 · Findings

What the evaluation supports.

01

Stronger ranking signal

The Random Forest improved ROC-AUC from 0.7583 to 0.8473 and Average Precision from 0.6583 to 0.8423.

02

Strong top-of-list concentration

Precision@10% reached 0.9878, compared with 0.6461 for Logistic Regression.

03

Observable bottlenecks

Click and engagement bottlenecks were the most frequently observed diagnostic conditions.

08 · Recommendations

A human-in-the-loop review workflow.

The ranking should guide where investigation begins, while editors retain responsibility for deciding whether an intervention is appropriate.

01

Start with Urgent Review

Review pages with model-estimated probability above 0.90 first and inspect their underlying bottlenecks.

02

Review High Priority next

Pages above 0.75 and up to 0.90 form the next review queue when capacity allows.

03

Investigate the bottleneck type

Use visibility, click, session, and engagement diagnostics to guide the human investigation.

04

Keep lower tiers in monitoring

Monitor lower-priority pages instead of automatically triggering content changes.

05

Validate with future outcomes

Post-refresh outcomes would allow stronger evaluation of whether prioritization predicts actual improvement.

09 · Limitations

What this model does not claim.

  • The priority target is a constructed proxy, not an observed refresh-needed label.
  • There is no verified post-refresh outcome in the available dataset.
  • The evaluation therefore cannot establish that refreshing prioritized pages will improve CTR, traffic, rankings, or engagement.
  • The project does not predict Google's ranking algorithm.
  • The test-set priority rate of 48.44% is higher than the training rate of 33.16%, creating distribution-shift uncertainty.
  • Tied model probabilities mean very small differences between adjacent ranks should not be treated as meaningful impact differences.

Final conclusion

The Random Forest provides a strong observed ranking signal for identifying pages that match the project's constructed bottleneck-based priority definition on the held-out client test set.

The result supports content-review prioritization, but should not be interpreted as evidence that refreshing prioritized pages will cause future performance improvement.
